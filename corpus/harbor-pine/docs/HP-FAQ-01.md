# Booking and rescheduling

## How do I book?
Book online through the customer portal or call the office on 555-0142 during business hours.

## Arrival windows
We give a 2-hour arrival window when you book. The technician texts you about 30 minutes before arriving.

## Can I reschedule?
Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours before the start of your arrival window. Changes made later are treated as late cancellations (see HP-POL-01).
