# Payments and invoices

## How can I pay?
We accept credit and debit cards and ACH bank transfer. We do not accept cash or cheques.

## When is payment due?
Payment is due when the job is completed. Business accounts with an agreement have 14 days to pay (net 14).

## Late payments
Invoices unpaid 30 days after the due date incur a late fee of 1.5% per month.

## Invoice copies
Copies of past invoices are available in the customer portal, or by asking support. We will never ask for your full card number by phone or email.
