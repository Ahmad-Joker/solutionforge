# Heating and cooling (HVAC) services

## Annual servicing
An annual HVAC service costs $149 per system. It includes a safety check, filter replacement, cleaning of the condensate drain and a written report.

## Repairs
HVAC repair visits use the standard $89 call-out fee, then $32 per 15 minutes of labour.

## New installations
We install furnaces, heat pumps and central air conditioning. Installation quotes are free, include a home survey, and are valid for 30 days. Installation labour is guaranteed under our 12-month workmanship warranty (HP-FAQ-03).

## Brands
We service all major brands. Manufacturer warranties on equipment are handled by the manufacturer; we help you file the claim.
