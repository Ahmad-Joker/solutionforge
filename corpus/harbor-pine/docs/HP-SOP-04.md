# Processing refund requests

## Checks
Confirm the job ID, the service date (the request must be within 30 days), and the reason against the refund policy (HP-POL-02).

## Approval
Support agents can approve refunds up to $500. Refunds over $500 need the service manager's approval.

## Paying the refund
Issue approved refunds to the original payment method within 5 business days and add a note to the ticket. Never refund to a different card or account than the one used to pay.
