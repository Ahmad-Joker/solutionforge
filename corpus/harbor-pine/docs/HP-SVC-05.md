# Service area and business hours

## Where we work
We serve Millhaven, Brookfield, Eastport and Cedar Falls: every address within 25 miles of our Millhaven depot. Addresses outside that radius are accepted only for HVAC installations, with a $60 travel surcharge.

## Business hours
Office and standard bookings: Monday to Friday 7:30 to 18:00, and Saturday 8:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays, except for the emergency line.

## Emergency line
The emergency line runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for the situations listed in HP-FAQ-05.
