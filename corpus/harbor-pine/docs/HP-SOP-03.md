# Technician arrival and safety checks

## On arrival
Show your ID badge, confirm the job with the customer, and put on shoe covers before entering.

## Before work
Isolate power before any electrical work and test that circuits are dead. Before any gas appliance work, run a leak test with the gas detector.

## Photos
Take before-and-after photos of the work area and attach them to the job record.

## Stop-work rule
Stop work and call the service manager if you find asbestos, unpermitted wiring, or a gas leak.
