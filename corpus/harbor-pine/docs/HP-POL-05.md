# Property damage and insurance claims

## Reporting damage
If you believe our work damaged your property, report it within 7 days of the visit, with photos.

## Our insurance
Harbor & Pine carries public liability insurance up to $1,000,000 per incident.

## What happens next
We acknowledge damage reports within 2 business days. A service manager inspects the damage, usually within 5 business days, and agrees a repair or a payment with you.
