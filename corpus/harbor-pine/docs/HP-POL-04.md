# Privacy and customer data

## What we keep
We keep job records, invoices and the contact details needed to deliver our services. Job records are kept for 6 years. Photos of completed work are kept for 2 years.

## What we never ask for
We will never ask for your full card number, passwords, or one-time codes by phone, text or email.

## Your rights
You can ask for a copy of your data, or for us to correct it, through the customer portal. We reply to data requests within 30 days.

## Sharing
We share data only with payment processors and with manufacturers when you ask us to file a warranty claim. We do not sell customer data.
