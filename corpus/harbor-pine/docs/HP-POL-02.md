# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Refund requests must be made within 30 days of the service date. We refund work that was not completed as quoted, and charges made in error.

## Call-out fees
Call-out fees are non-refundable, except when we arrived more than 60 minutes after the end of your arrival window.

## Unused parts
Unopened parts returned within 14 days are refunded, minus a 10% restocking fee.

## How refunds are paid
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days of approval. We do not refund in cash.
