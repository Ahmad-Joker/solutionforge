# Handling emergency calls

## Gas smell
If a caller reports a gas smell, tell them to leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not book a technician until the utility has made the site safe.

## Flooding
Ask the caller to turn off the main water valve if they can do so safely. Dispatch the nearest plumber as urgent.

## Electrical burning smell
Ask the caller to switch off the main breaker only if it is safe and dry to reach it. Dispatch an electrician as urgent.

## Record keeping
Every emergency call gets an urgent ticket before the call ends.
