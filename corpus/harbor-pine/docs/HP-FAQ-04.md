# What to expect on the day of your visit

## Before arrival
The technician texts you about 30 minutes before arrival with their name and photo.

## Identification
Every technician carries a Harbor & Pine photo ID badge. Ask to see it before letting anyone in.

## In your home
Technicians wear shoe covers indoors, protect floors in the work area, and take before-and-after photos of the work for your job record.

## Someone must be home
An adult aged 18 or over must be present for the whole visit.
