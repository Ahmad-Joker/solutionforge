# Electrical services

## What we do
Our licensed electricians install lighting, outlets and ceiling fans, fix tripping breakers, upgrade electrical panels and install EV chargers.

## EV chargers
Level 2 EV charger installation starts at $1,150, including a standard 30-foot cable run and the permit application. Longer runs or panel upgrades are quoted separately.

## Panel upgrades
Panel upgrades are priced by quote after a site survey. The survey is free.

## Permits
We obtain the local permits that electrical work requires. We do not connect to wiring that was installed without a permit until it has been inspected.
