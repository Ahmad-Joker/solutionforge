# Plumbing services and call-out fees

## What we do
Harbor & Pine plumbers repair leaks, unblock drains, replace taps and toilets, fit water heaters and trace low water pressure. We do not work on mains water supply pipes owned by the water utility.

## Call-out fee
A standard plumbing visit costs an $89 call-out fee, which covers travel and the first 30 minutes of labour. After that, labour is billed at $32 per 15 minutes. Parts are charged at the price shown on your quote.

## Out-of-hours visits
Visits outside business hours use the emergency call-out fee of $189, which covers the first 30 minutes. See the emergency service FAQ (HP-FAQ-05) for what counts as an emergency.

## Water heaters
We install tank and tankless water heaters. A replacement water heater quote is free and valid for 30 days.
