# Emergency service

## What counts as an emergency?
Burst pipes or flooding, sewage backing up, no heat when the outdoor temperature is below 40°F, and a burning smell from electrical outlets or panels.

## If you smell gas
Leave the building immediately and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not use switches or phones inside. Call us only after the utility says it is safe.

## Response time
Our target is to have a technician on site within 2 hours of your call for addresses in our core service area.

## Price
Emergency visits outside business hours use the $189 emergency call-out fee (HP-SVC-01).
