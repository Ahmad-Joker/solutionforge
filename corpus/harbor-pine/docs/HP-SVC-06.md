# Pine Plus maintenance membership

## Price
Pine Plus costs $14.99 per month, or $149 per year if paid annually.

## Benefits
- One annual HVAC service per year included (normally $149).
- The $89 call-out fee is waived on standard plumbing and HVAC repair visits.
- 15% off labour on all services.
- Priority booking: a visit within 48 hours on weekdays.

## Cancelling
Monthly memberships can be cancelled at any time and end at the close of the current month. Annual memberships cancelled within the first 60 days are refunded pro rata, minus the value of any benefits already used. After 60 days, annual memberships are not refundable.
