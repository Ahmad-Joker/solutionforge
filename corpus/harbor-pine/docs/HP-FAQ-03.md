# Workmanship warranty

## What is covered?
Our labour is guaranteed for 12 months from the completion date. If a repair we made fails because of our workmanship in that time, we fix it at no charge.

## Parts
Parts are covered by the manufacturer's warranty, which varies by product. We help you file manufacturer claims.

## Cleaning
If you are unhappy with a clean, report it within 48 hours and we will re-clean the affected areas free of charge.

## Exclusions
The warranty does not cover damage caused by misuse, by other contractors, or by parts you supplied yourself.
