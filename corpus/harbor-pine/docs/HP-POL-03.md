# Complaints and escalation

## Making a complaint
You can complain through the customer portal, by email, or by phone. Each complaint gets a support ticket.

## Our response
We reply to every complaint within 2 business days.

## Escalation
If a complaint is not resolved within 5 business days, it is escalated to the service manager. If you are still not satisfied, you can ask for a final review by the operations director, who responds within 10 business days.
