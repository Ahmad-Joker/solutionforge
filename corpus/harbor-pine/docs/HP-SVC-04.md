# Home cleaning plans

## Standard clean
A standard clean costs $45 per cleaner-hour, with a 2-hour minimum. It covers dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen surfaces and bathrooms.

## Deep clean
A deep clean costs $60 per cleaner-hour, with a 3-hour minimum. It adds inside the oven, inside the fridge, baseboards and interior windows.

## Recurring plans
Weekly and bi-weekly recurring plans get 10% off the hourly rate. You can pause a recurring plan for up to 4 weeks at no charge.

## Supplies
Cleaners bring all supplies and equipment. Tell us in advance if you prefer fragrance-free products.
