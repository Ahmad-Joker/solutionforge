# Support ticket triage and priorities

## Ticket IDs
Every support ticket has an ID in the format T- followed by 8 characters (digits 0-9 or letters A-F), for example T-1A2B3C4D.

## Priorities and response targets
- Urgent: a safety risk (gas smell, electrical burning smell, active flooding). First response within 1 hour, at any time.
- High: no heat, no cooling or no water. First response within 4 business hours.
- Normal: everything else that needs a visit. First response within 1 business day.
- Low: questions, invoice copies and feedback. First response within 3 business days.

## Statuses
Tickets move through open, in_progress, waiting_on_customer and closed.
