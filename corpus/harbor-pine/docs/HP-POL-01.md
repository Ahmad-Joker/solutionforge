# Cancellation policy

## Free cancellation
You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the start of your arrival window.

## Late cancellation
Cancellations less than 24 hours before the arrival window cost $40. For cleaning bookings, late cancellations cost 50% of the booked hours.

## No access
If nobody is home or we cannot get access when the technician arrives, the full $89 call-out fee is charged.

## If we cancel
If we cancel or miss your arrival window by more than 60 minutes, your next call-out fee is waived.
